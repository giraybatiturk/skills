# Idea generation techniques

Evidence behind these rules: multi-step prompting (draft, critique to make bolder, expand) produced the most diverse LLM ideas; independent generation beats building on each other's ideas; ordinary concrete personas spread ideas more than famous-innovator personas; later ideas tend to be more original than early ones. Treat these as design choices to re-test, not guarantees.

## Loop for every generator

1. **Draft** 10–12 ideas quickly.
2. **Make bolder:** critique the draft for obviousness and rewrite the weakest half to be less expected while still serving the frame.
3. **Expand:** give each surviving idea one sentence: what the user does or gets, and why it matters to them.

## Technique sets (one set per generator in Deep mode)

**Set A: structure**
- *Mind map:* put the frame in the centre; branch by user need, moment of use, channel and constraint; generate ideas at the leaves.
- *SCAMPER:* Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other use, Eliminate, Reverse applied to the current product or workflow.

**Set B: inversion**
- *Reverse brainstorming:* list ways to make the problem worse or guarantee failure, then invert each into an idea.
- *Assumption reversal:* list 5 assumptions the product or market takes for granted; flip each.
- *Constraint removal and addition:* what if budget, time or the main technical limit disappeared; what if a severe new limit appeared.

**Set C: people**
- *Role storming with ordinary personas:* 2–3 concrete people specific to the problem (for a medication tracker: a night-shift nurse on GLP-1 treatment, a retired parent managing three prescriptions, a caregiver checking remotely). Generate as each person, in their words, from their day.
- *Starbursting (5W1H):* who, what, where, when, why, how questions about the frame; each unanswered question becomes an idea.
- *Adjacent domain:* borrow a pattern from another industry; name the source and mark it unverified until checked.

## Pooling

Merge the three outputs, remove near-duplicates (same user outcome in different words), and keep 25–30. Prefer keeping later, less obvious ideas when trimming. Keep the obvious baseline in the report as the idea to beat.
