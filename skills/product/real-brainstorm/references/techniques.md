# Idea generation techniques

Evidence behind these rules:
- Multi-step prompting (draft, critique to make bolder, expand) produced the most diverse LLM ideas.
- Independent generation beats building on each other's ideas.
- Ordinary concrete personas spread ideas more than famous-innovator personas.
- Later ideas tend to be more original than early ideas.

Treat these as design choices to test again. They are not guarantees.

## Loop for every generator

1. **Draft** 10–12 ideas quickly.
2. **Make bolder:** critique the draft for obviousness. Rewrite the weakest half to be less expected, and keep it useful for the frame.
3. **Expand:** give each surviving idea one sentence: what the user does or gets, and why it matters to the user.

## Technique sets (one set per generator in Deep mode)

**Set A: structure**
- *Mind map:* put the frame in the centre. Branch by user need, moment of use, channel and constraint. Generate ideas at the leaves.
- *SCAMPER:* Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other use, Eliminate, Reverse. Apply them to the current product or workflow.

**Set B: inversion**
- *Reverse brainstorming:* list ways to make the problem worse or to guarantee failure. Then invert each one into an idea.
- *Assumption reversal:* list 5 assumptions that the product or market takes for granted. Flip each one.
- *Constraint removal and addition:* what if the budget, time or the main technical limit disappeared? What if a severe new limit appeared?

**Set C: people**
- *Role storming with ordinary personas:* use 2–3 concrete people who are specific to the problem. For a medication tracker, for example: a night-shift nurse on GLP-1 treatment, a retired parent who manages three prescriptions, a caregiver who checks remotely. Generate as each person, in their words, from their day.
- *Starbursting (5W1H):* ask who, what, where, when, why and how questions about the frame. Each unanswered question becomes an idea.
- *Adjacent domain:* borrow a pattern from another industry. Name the source. Mark it unverified until you verify it.

## Pooling

Merge the three outputs. Delete near-duplicates (same user outcome in different words). Keep 25–30.
- When you trim, prefer to keep later, less obvious ideas.
- Keep the obvious baseline in the report as the idea to beat.
