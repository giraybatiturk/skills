# Market card

Read Real Audit `references/monetization.md` (Pricing evaluation and Evidence sections) read-only for the value, willingness-to-pay and measurement rules. This file adds only the forward-looking parts for ideas that do not exist yet. Every claim carries a URL and access date, or the label [Varsayım]/unverified.

## Per candidate

1. **Existing products:** who already does this (direct and adjacent), their price and package, and how users describe them in reviews. If nobody does, say so and name where you searched; absence in search is not proof of no competitor.
2. **Precedents:** similar features or products that succeeded or failed, and the stated reason. Precedent suggests, it does not prove.
3. **Customer and willingness to pay:** who pays (user, employer, insurer), signals of willingness to pay (competitor prices, paid tiers in the category, price complaints in reviews). A competitor price is a reference point, not this product's willingness to pay.
4. **Revenue range:** a Fermi estimate with each input written out (reachable users, conversion, price, period) as low / base / high. Label the result [Varsayım]. Do not give a single number and do not present it as a forecast. If the product has real baseline data (installs, paywall views, conversion), use it and cite it.
5. **Cost and effort:** rough build and running cost, and dependencies (third parties, approvals, policy such as App Store or health-data rules, checked against current sources).
6. **Cheapest test:** the smallest action that would move the revenue range or kill the idea (for example a fake-door button, a landing page with a price, five customer calls). State what result would count as a pass.

## Verdict line

One sentence per candidate: likely to work / uncertain / unlikely, and the single fact that would change that verdict.
